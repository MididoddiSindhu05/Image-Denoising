#Image-Denoising 
Overview

This project was carried out during my Research Internship at the National Institute of Technology (NIT) Warangal under the guidance of Dr. Manish Kumar Bajpayee. The research focused on enhancing image quality through deep learning-based denoising techniques for computer vision applications.

The project involved preprocessing noisy image datasets, training and evaluating deep learning models, and optimizing model performance to improve image clarity while preserving important visual details.
